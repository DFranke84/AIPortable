Optional — only needed for the mic/voice-input feature.

Place a whisper.cpp server build here:
  resources/whisper/mac/whisper-server
  resources/whisper/mac/model.bin   (e.g. a ggml-base.en.bin model, renamed)

Get binaries/models from: https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp
Without these, the mic button just reports "unavailable" — everything else
in the app works fine.
